Four families of mental shortcuts

Grouping the Cognitive Bias Codex by why the brain fails, Buster Benson sorts the biases into four mechanisms: too much information, not enough meaning, the need to act fast, and the limits of memory. Every category page can be expanded entry by entry.

From knowing to doing: debiasing is not a slogan

Research shows that simply learning about a bias produces only short-lived alertness. Repeated training, structured decision procedures and techniques such as the premortem are what deliver measurable, lasting effects.
