A guided AI workflow that supports teachers of Indigenous languages in teaching the language: it helps create materials and lesson plans based on the teacher's own materials and knowledge—grounded in source context, clear evidence, and appropriate limits on inference.

How to use it

  1. 01 Give the task and the language or variety.
  2. 02 Provide sources, audience, orthography, and constraints.
  3. 03 Review language forms and cultural claims with appropriate authority.

Example: “Use this skill to create a lesson plan and teaching materials from my own sources and knowledge. Preserve the supplied orthography, cite documented claims, and flag items requiring fluent-speaker or community review.”
