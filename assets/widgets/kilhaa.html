A language learning game

kiłáa
An Umatilla word game.

kiłáa is an object-sorting vocabulary game for Umatilla. Each room shows a cluttered scene of objects to be matched into the right collection boxes; English appears for the first three boxes of every word, and each Umatilla word links out to its entry in the CTUIR online dictionary.

Play kiłáa

Project format

A room-by-room word game

22 rooms · 250 objects per level · hints

How to play it

  1. 01 Tap an object in the room, then match it to the correct collection box.
  2. 02 Learn the ten words in each room — no word repeats across the 22 rooms.
  3. 03 Spend 10 points on a hint if a word stalls you; progress stays saved on your device.
✦

Attribution note: vocabulary is taken from the Confederated Tribes of the Umatilla Indian Reservation Umatilla Language Online Dictionary. Entries have not been independently reviewed by a fluent speaker for this game, and the visuals are illustrative rather than culturally authoritative.
